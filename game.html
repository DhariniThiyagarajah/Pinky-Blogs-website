Pinky Maze Chase

Collect every heart dot, avoid the wandering spirits, and use the large hearts to make them vulnerable.

How to play

  • Use arrow keys or WASD to move.
  • Collect all dots to complete the level.
  • Large hearts activate power mode.
  • Catch pink spirits only during power mode.
